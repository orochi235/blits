# A smaller bundle

<p class="lede">`@msb235/blits/lean` is the same library without lanes. Every export is there and every pose comes out the same; only the code that works a channel out for every subject at once is left out.</p>

```ts
import { kit, mix, patch, sum } from '@msb235/blits/lean';
```

## What lanes are

A mix can fold a channel two ways. The general path works out a subject's pose when you probe that subject. A **lane** works out the channel for every subject at once, in flat arrays, at the frame's first probe. A channel runs as a lane when every voice writing it can, and the answer is the same either way. Lanes pay off when a frame reads hundreds or thousands of subjects, and most of all through `pull`.

Some voices never run as a lane, so a mix full of them gets nothing from the lane code:

- A voice weighted by a signal marked as an input (one that reads something the clock doesn't drive) that `input` did not make. `input` makes one that reports its changes, which keeps its voice on a lane.
- A voice whose patch `reads` fields of the host.

## When to use it

A host that probes tens of subjects a frame, such as a page of DOM elements or a few meshes, doesn't need lanes. Import from `@msb235/blits/lean` everywhere in the app; a single import from `@msb235/blits` brings the full build back into the bundle.

What changes:

- `mix` makes a mix on the `lean` engine, which folds every voice subject by subject.
- `lanes: true` in `MixOptions` throws. Leaving `lanes` out, or passing `false`, is fine.
- `mixer`, the full engine, is still exported, and passing it as `engine` brings the lanes back.

To compare the two in your own app, keep the full import and pass `lanes: false`: it runs the same general path the lean engine does.

## How much smaller

Measured on the 0.13.0 source, bundling an entry that imports `kit`, `mix`, `patch`, and `sum` with esbuild 0.28.2 (`--bundle --minify --format=esm`), then `gzip -9`:

| Import from | Minified | Gzipped |
|---|---:|---:|
| `@msb235/blits` | 202.2 kB | 70.2 kB |
| `@msb235/blits/lean` | 157.5 kB | 54.2 kB |

About a fifth to a quarter less. Your bundler and what else you import will move both numbers.
