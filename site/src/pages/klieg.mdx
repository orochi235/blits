# klieg

<p class="lede">blits was extracted from <a href="https://github.com/orochi235/klieg">klieg</a>, a neon-sign renderer, which now runs its motion, its effects and its lighting on three mixes.</p>

Each of the three is a kit, a subject type and a mix over them:

| Mix | Subjects | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| Motion | Each letter | `position` and `rotation` (`vec(3, sum())`), `scale` and `opacity` (`mul`) |
| Effects | Each part of the sign | `gain`, `scale` (`mul`), `dark` (`max`), `crawl` (`sum`), `position`, `rotation`, `light` (`vec(3, sum())`), `color` (`last`) |
| Lighting | One subject: the whole frame | `yaw` and `pitch` (`sum`) |

The port was an extraction, not a rewrite. Its first step reproduced klieg's arithmetic exactly: no number changed, klieg's own test suites pass as they did, and none of its Playwright visual baselines moved.

A live sign goes here, with its three mixes exposed so you can watch each voice's weight and what it contributes. It isn't built yet: it waits until klieg depends on the published package rather than on a local copy of this repo.
