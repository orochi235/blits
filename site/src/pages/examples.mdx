# Examples

<p class="lede">Small effects, each running above its full source. Every one is a single file you could copy into a page of your own, wrapped in a <code>run</code> function so this page can start and stop it. Each runs on the <code>loop</code> from <a href="../start/#on-dom-elements">Get started</a>, which pauses while the window is out of focus.</p>

## Staggered text reveal

Each letter is a subject. One voice reaches all of them, and its `stagger` delays each letter by its place in the line. `freeze` holds a letter's first frame before its turn and its last frame after, until the voice fades out. See [Voice](../voice/).

## Hover glow

Point at a card. One voice holds every card raised and glowing, and its weight is a [signal](../signal/): an `input` that reads which card the pointer is over, smoothed by `lag` so each card rises quickly and settles slowly.

## Particle burst

Click the canvas. Each burst is one voice over 40 new sparks, played for one pass. When the pass ends the voice leaves the mix, its `done` promise resolves, and the sparks are let go. See [Mix](../mix/).

## Crossfade between loops

Press the button. The playing loop fades out as the other fades in, and both share a [role](../role/), so halfway through, each dot is a blend of the two: its color passes from one to the other rather than stacking both.

## A scrubbable sequence

Drag the slider. Three voices are placed on the mix's [score](../score/): the second starts 300 ms after the first reaches its `coast` mark, where it begins holding its last frame, and the third starts 250 ms after the second. The mix itself never moves past 0. Each frame, `project` reads it at the slider's time without moving it, so scrubbing backward needs no history.
