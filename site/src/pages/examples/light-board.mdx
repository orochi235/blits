# Light board

A stage set lit the way a lighting desk lights one, every fixture a voice. Each look cues a gel on `color(colorMode())`, which keeps the set's lightness and gives it the gel's hue and chroma; spots on `color(colorDodge())`, which brighten what is under them more where it is already lit; and for the blackout, a master on `color(multiply())` that multiplies everything down to black. The frame loop lays the three channels on the set in that order with `toHex`.

Changing look fades the last look's voices out and the new one's in. The gels share a [role](../../role/), so one gel crossfades into the next through OKLab rather than the two stacking. Run the show steps through the looks on the transport's clock; Pause sets its `rate` to 0, and the [debug panel](../../guides/debugging/) can seek back through the last four looks.
