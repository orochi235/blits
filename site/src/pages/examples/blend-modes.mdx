# Blend modes

A disc of blue sits between two bands of orange, over a ramp from black to white through muted color, and it sweeps left and right on a looping voice. The thumbnails come from one mix holding every mode, and the large view from a second mix holding only the mode it shows, since a probe pays for every channel in its kit; both run on one [`transport`](../../mix/#api-transport). Pause sets the transport's `rate` to 0, and dragging the slider seeks each mix's disc voice to that point in its sweep. The bottom band is cued before the disc, so it lies underneath, and the top band after it, so it lies on top. Each [blend mode](../../channel/#blend-modes) folds the same three voices, and `toHex(pose, cell.base)` lays the result on the ramp; click a thumbnail to see that mode large. In the modes marked ordered, the disc's two crossings differ, because each layer works on what is under it; in the rest they look alike. Hue and saturation keep what is under them where it is gray, since gray has no hue, so they show only where the ramp has color.
