# Examples

<p class="lede">Small effects, each running above its full source. Every one is a single file you could copy into a page of your own, wrapped in a <code>run</code> function so this page can start and stop it. Each runs on the <code>loop</code> from <a href="../start/#on-dom-elements">Get started</a>, which pauses while the window is out of focus.</p>

## Staggered text reveal

Each letter is a subject. One voice reaches all of them, and its [`stagger`](VoiceSpec.stagger) delays each letter by its place in the line. [`freeze`](VoiceSpec.freeze) holds a letter's first frame before its turn and its last frame after, until the voice fades out. See [Voice](../voice/).

## Hover glow

Point at a card. One voice holds every card raised and glowing, and its weight is a [signal](../signal/): an `input` that reads which card the pointer is over, smoothed by `lag` so each card rises quickly and settles slowly.

## Particle burst

Click the canvas. Each burst is one voice over 40 new sparks, played for one pass. When the pass ends the voice leaves the mix, its [`done`](Handle.done) promise resolves, and the sparks are let go. See [Mix](../mix/).

## Crossfade between loops

Press the button. The playing loop fades out as the other fades in, and both share a [role](../role/), so halfway through, each dot is a blend of the two: its color passes from one to the other rather than stacking both.

## A scrubbable sequence

Drag the slider. Three voices are placed on the mix's [score](../score/): the second starts 300 ms after the first reaches its `coast` mark, where it begins holding its last frame, and the third starts 250 ms after the second. The mix plays on its own clock and [`seek`](Mix.seek)s back to 0 at the end. While the slider is held, the mix isn't synced, so the slider can seek it anywhere in its history; letting go calls [`rebase`](Mix.rebase) and play resumes from there.

## On pages of their own

- [Blend modes](./blend-modes/): one disc between two bands, folded by each of the color blend modes.
- [Number rules](./number-rules/): the same three layers as numbers, folded by each number channel's rule.
- [Order matters](./stack-order/): three discs cued in two orders under one blend mode, and where the two differ.
- [Light board](./light-board/): a stage lit by voices, a gel on `colorMode`, spots on `colorDodge`, and a master on `multiply`.
- [Retheme](./retheme/): a mock interface's color tokens through one `hue` voice, recolored without losing contrast.
- [Threshold wipe](./threshold-wipe/): a hard-edged wipe from one voice on `hardMix` over a map.
- [Hit flash](./hit-flash/): each hit one short voice that inverts a creature for a moment.
- [White balance](./white-balance/): a colored light on `multiply`, corrected by a voice on `divide` by the same color.
