# Number rules

The same three layers as numbers: the bottom band writes 0.5, the disc 0.9, and the top band 0.7, and each [number channel](../../channel/) folds them by its own rule. The color scale runs from 0 to 2. Where no voice writes, each rule shows its rest: 0 for `sum`, `max`, `norm`, and `screen`, and 1 for `mul` and `min`. `last` has no rest, so a cell no voice reaches has no value, drawn gray, and where they overlap it takes the voice cued last. `norm` sits between `sum` and `max`, closer to `max` as its power rises: the disc over the bottom band reads 1.40 under `sum`, 1.03 under `norm(2)`, 0.92 under `norm(4)`, and 0.90 under `max`. Pause and drag the slider to stop the disc where it overlaps a band.
