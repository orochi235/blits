# Threshold wipe

The picture under the voice is a map: light where the wipe should arrive first, dark where it should arrive last. One looping voice on `color(hardMix())` writes a single gray that rises from black to white and back. `hardMix` turns each channel full on where the map and the gray add up to 1 or more, and off where they don't, so the gray works as a threshold and the edge between on and off follows the map's contours as the gray rises. Each of the map's channels is a little apart from the others, so the edge passes through color on its way. The wipe's shape and timing are all in the map and the voice; the frame loop only syncs, probes and draws.

`vividLight` and `linearLight` fold the same voice into a soft edge: they ramp where `hardMix` steps. Pause sets the transport's `rate` to 0, and dragging the slider seeks the voice.
