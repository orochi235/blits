# Order matters

Three discs turn about the middle of a ramp, each a looping voice that writes one [color channel](../../channel/#blend-modes). The left mix cues them A, B, C, and the right mix cues them in the order shown under it; swap neighbors to change it. Both mixes run on one [`transport`](../../mix/#api-transport), and a change makes them again with each voice cued as far into its turn as the transport is.

Under `multiply`, `screen`, `add`, `lighten` and `darken`, the layers fold into one before anything is laid on the ramp, so the order can't change the color, and the readout stays at 0%. Under the ordered modes, `toHex` lays each voice on what is under it, first cued at the bottom, so swapping two voices changes every pixel where they overlap. `exclusion` is ordered but reads 0% too: laying one exclusion on another gives the same color whichever goes first.
