# Follows 2026-11-12-gpu-cue-levels.txt. A timing block's level codes past 64 KB (32,768 subjects)
# go up once into a GPU buffer of their own, and wherever the block is laid in `persub` they are
# copied there by the frame's command encoder (`copyBufferToBuffer`), not sent from the CPU. For a
# voice cued with a named stagger the buffer is made at cue, through the same `delayed` hook, so
# the frame it starts in sends nothing per subject; for any other the first write makes it. A
# block moved by another ending is copied again on the GPU. Each buffer is destroyed once its voice
# is done or its block lays other levels, and all of them at `close`.
